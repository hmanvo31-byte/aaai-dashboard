About this dashboard

This prototype brings together two source workbooks: a cross-country demographic basic-information sheet, and a synthesized set of 65 policy & statistics indicators across six domains (policy & statistics, income & livelihood security, health & well-being, social capital & participation, enabling environment, and digital inclusion).

How status is determined

Each indicator's "Value (Final)" cell is read and classified as Yes, No, Partial, or a quantitative figure. Because the source data was entered by different country teams in free text, this classification is a best-effort heuristic, not an authoritative score.

Known data quality issues to resolve before publishing:
  • Some "Value (Final)" cells contain a source URL instead of Yes/No (several Japan entries) — the dashboard currently treats a bare link as "Yes, with a caveat," but this should be corrected at the source.
  • A few cells still read "Not sure", "TBC", "Confirming" or are blank — these show as Partial or unreported rather than a real answer.
  • The original workbook contains a small number of #REF! formula errors on the "never married/single" row that should be fixed before this feeds a public site.

Updating the data

Everything on this page is read from data.js. To publish new figures: update the country tabs in the source workbook, re-run the export step (see README), and replace data.js — no changes to index.html, styles.css or app.js are needed for a routine data refresh.
