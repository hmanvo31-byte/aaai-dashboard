How to use this dashboard

  • Overview — the regional snapshot: total population 60+, average dependency ratio, the overall composite index (pending), a quick card for each country, and Resources — the guidebook, background reading, and how to contribute data for other ASEAN member states.
  • Map — where the countries sit geographically, with circle size showing population 60+. Click a country to jump to its profile.
  • Country profiles — pick a country on the left to see its living arrangements, marital status, a policy scorecard by domain, and recommendations specific to that country.
  • Domain — the full 62-indicator table, a domain composite index (pending), and recommendations specific to that domain. Filter to gaps only, search by name, or click any row to see every country's answer, year, and source.
  • Infographic by domain — a domain-level visual comparison across all six countries, plus drill-down into any single indicator.

About this dashboard

Comparative index across 6 domains — 62 AAAI indicators plus 18 basic demographic indicators — for older persons aged 60+ across 5 ASEAN member states and Japan as a reference country. This prototype brings together two source workbooks: a cross-country demographic basic-information sheet, and a synthesized set of policy & statistics indicators across six domains (policy & statistics, income & livelihood security, health & well-being, social capital & participation, enabling environment, and digital inclusion).

How status is determined

Each indicator's "Value (Final)" cell is read and classified as Yes, No, Pending, or a quantitative figure. Pending covers both blank cells and answers still being confirmed ("Not sure", "TBC") — it means data collection isn't finished yet, not that the policy itself is partial. Because the source data was entered by different country teams in free text, this classification is a best-effort heuristic, not an authoritative score.

Known data quality issues to resolve before publishing:
  • Some "Value (Final)" cells contain a source URL instead of Yes/No (several Japan entries) — the dashboard currently treats a bare link as "Yes, with a caveat," but this should be corrected at the source.
  • A few cells still read "Not sure", "TBC", "Confirming" or are blank — these now show as Pending rather than a real answer.
  • The original workbook contains a small number of #REF! formula errors on the "never married/single" row that should be fixed before this feeds a public site.
  • The "Year" shown for policy indicators reflects the year of the originally-cited source, which in some rows was later superseded by a different "Source (Final)" — the workbook doesn't carry a separate finalized year, so treat the year as approximate until reconciled.

Updating the data

Everything on this page is read from data.js. To publish new figures: update the country tabs in the source workbook, re-run the export step (see README), and replace data.js — no changes to index.html, styles.css or app.js are needed for a routine data refresh.
